## 🚀 What It Is
GPU Overclocking Tool for Windows is a hands-on utility for gpu overclocking on hardware you own. It gives you a clear place to adjust core and memory clocks, tune the power target, and design a fan curve, all while monitoring temperature and clock speeds. Overclocking a gpu can improve performance in some tasks, but it also increases heat and power draw and can affect stability, so the app is designed to help you make small, testable changes at your own pace and your own risk.

## ✨ Key Features
> Everything you need to explore what overclocking gpu can do on your own card — tune, watch, and validate, one step at a time.

- 🎚️ **Clock Tuning** — Apply core and memory clock offsets in small increments you can reverse whenever you like.
- ⚡ **Power Control** — Adjust the power limit inside your card's supported range to shape how hard it can work.
- 🌀 **Fan Curves** — Tie fan speed to temperature so cooling responds as the card warms under load.
- 📊 **Live Monitoring** — Keep clocks, temperatures, power, and utilization on screen as you experiment.
- 🧪 **Stress Testing** — Load a saved profile and hold it to confirm stability before daily use.

## ❓ Questions
**Is GPU Overclocking Tool free?**
Yes, a free version is available, so you can start tuning your card at no cost. Check the official download for exactly what the free version covers.

**What is gpu overclocking, and what does it do?**
Gpu overclocking means running your card's core and memory above their default clock speeds to try for more performance. In practice, what overclocking gpu does depends on your specific card, cooling, and workload — it can help, but it also raises heat and power use and can affect stability, so any improvement is not guaranteed.

**Is overclocking gpu safe, and does overclocking reduce gpu lifespan?**
Overclocking gpu can be reasonably safe when you stay within your card's limits and monitor temperatures, but it carries real trade-offs. Extra heat, power, and voltage can affect stability and may reduce lifespan over time, and it can void your warranty. Move in small steps, keep an eye on the sensors, and accept that you tune at your own risk.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
GPU Overclocking Tool runs on Windows and targets current 64-bit releases such as Windows 10 and Windows 11.
